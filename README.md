# 📊 ConnectX Telecom Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn behavior for a telecom company using Power BI. The objective is to identify the factors contributing to customer attrition and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention and maximize customer lifetime value.

The dashboard explores customer demographics, contract types, payment methods, tenure, revenue, and churn patterns to uncover key business insights.

---

## Business Problem

Customer churn is a major challenge in the telecommunications industry. Acquiring new customers is significantly more expensive than retaining existing ones. Understanding why customers leave and identifying at-risk customer segments can help organizations develop effective retention strategies and improve profitability.

## Key Insights

### 1. Customer Churn Rate

- 28.37% of customers have churned.
- 71.63% of customers remain active.
- Nearly 3 out of every 10 customers leave the company.

### 2. Revenue Contribution by Gender

- Revenue generation is nearly identical between male and female customers.
- Male customers contribute approximately 50.6% of total revenue.
- Female customers contribute approximately 49.4% of total revenue.
- Gender does not appear to be a major factor affecting revenue.

### 3. Contract Type Impact

- Monthly contracts are the most popular contract type.
- Customers with One-Year and Two-Year contracts demonstrate significantly lower churn rates.
- Long-term contracts are associated with stronger customer retention.

### 4. Customer Tenure & Churn

- Customers who stayed have an average tenure of approximately 42 months.
- Customers who churned have an average tenure of approximately 17 months.
- A potential churn-risk period exists around months 17–18.

### 5. Revenue & Customer Lifetime Value

- Total charges increase as customer tenure increases.
- Long-term customers generate substantially higher revenue.
- Customer retention directly impacts profitability.

### 6. Age & Churn Relationship

- Churned customers tend to be older, with an average age close to 50 years.
- Older customer segments may require tailored products and services.

---

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Launch Proactive Retention Campaigns

Implement targeted retention programs before customers reach the 17–18 month tenure period.

### 2. Promote Long-Term Contracts

Encourage customers to migrate from monthly plans to annual contracts through discounts, bundled services, and loyalty rewards.

### 3. Strengthen Customer Loyalty Programs

Reward long-tenure customers with exclusive benefits to increase customer satisfaction and reduce churn.

### 4. Improve Service Quality

Enhance network reliability, customer support responsiveness, and overall customer experience.

### 5. Develop Senior-Focused Offerings

Create products, plans, and support services tailored to older customer segments.

### 6. Implement Predictive Churn Analytics

Leverage machine learning models to identify high-risk customers and proactively intervene before churn occurs.

---

## Business Impact

The insights generated from this analysis can help telecom providers:

- Reduce customer churn
- Improve customer retention
- Increase customer lifetime value
- Lower customer acquisition costs
- Enhance profitability

Since retaining existing customers is generally more cost-effective than acquiring new customers, data-driven retention strategies can significantly improve business performance.
